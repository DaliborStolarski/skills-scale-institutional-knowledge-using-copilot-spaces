# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
OctoAcme uses a lightweight but structured project management approach designed to keep teams aligned from project initiation through execution, release, and continuous improvement. The process emphasizes clear ownership, iterative delivery, proactive communication, measurable outcomes, and learning from each project cycle.

## Project Management Processes
The OctoAcme model follows a practical lifecycle:

- **Initiation**: validate the business need, align stakeholders, define success metrics, and decide whether to move forward.
- **Planning**: turn the approved initiative into a clear backlog, milestone plan, and delivery approach with explicit ownership and dependencies.
- **Execution & Tracking**: manage day-to-day delivery with regular standups, progress tracking, quality checks, and escalation paths for blockers.
- **Risk & Communication**: monitor risks, maintain communication rhythms, and escalate issues early to keep stakeholders informed.
- **Release & Deployment**: prepare for deployment, validate quality, and coordinate rollout and operational readiness.
- **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement**: capture lessons learned, identify improvements, and update how the team works going forward.

This approach balances accountability, transparency, and adaptability so projects can move quickly without losing visibility or quality.
